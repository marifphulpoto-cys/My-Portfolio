🛫 How to Book a Flight — Step by Step Guide

  • Step 1: Search flights using your departure and destination cities, along with dates.
  • Step 2: Choose your desired flight based on time, price, and airline.
  • Step 3: Fill in passenger details including full name, DOB, nationality, and contact info.
  • Step 4: Select extras like baggage, meals, or seat preferences if available.
  • Step 5: Make payment through secure methods (Card, Mobile wallet, etc.).
  • Step 6: You will receive a confirmation with ticket and flight details via email.
  • Step 7: On travel day, arrive early at the airport with valid ID and booking info.
  • Step 8: After flight, follow signs for baggage claim or exit gates.

🧾 If you face any issues during the process or while booking, feel free to contact our help desk or submit an e-complaint.
