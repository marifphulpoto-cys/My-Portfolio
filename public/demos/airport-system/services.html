🧳 Services & Facilities at Musavi Airport

Explore the range of world-class services designed to make your airport experience smooth and enjoyable.

🛋️ Lounges & Comfort

  • Premium Lounges: Quiet zones with snacks, Wi-Fi, and workstations.
  • Rest Zones: Sleep pods and recliner chairs available for long layovers.

🍽️ Food & Beverages

  • Food Courts: Wide range of local and international cuisines.
  • 24/7 Cafés: Hot meals, coffee, and snacks round the clock.

🛍️ Shopping & Duty-Free

  • Duty-Free: Perfumes, gadgets, and souvenirs at tax-free rates.
  • Retail Stores: Everything from books to local crafts.

📋 Ticketing & Flight Info

  • Airline Desks: Book or update flights at all major counters.
  • Flight Displays: Real-time screens across the terminal.

🛡️ Safety & Assistance

  • Medical Center: 24/7 doctor and first-aid services.
  • Wheelchair Support: Free service on request.
  • Help Desks: For elderly and specially-abled travelers.

🚕 Transport & Parking

  • Taxi & Shuttle: Convenient airport transfers available anytime.
  • Rental Cars: On-spot bookings from top providers.
  • Parking Zones: Short- and long-term secured parking.

📶 Digital Facilities

  • Free Wi-Fi: Strong signal across all terminals.
  • Charging Spots: Easily found in lounges and gates.

🧳 Baggage Help

  • Trolleys: Free and available at all key points.
  • Lost & Found: Located near Gate 3.

🕌 Family & Spiritual Needs

  • Prayer Rooms: Male and female facilities available.
  • Baby Care: Diaper stations and family waiting zones.

Need help? Ask our support desks inside the terminal or submit an e-complaint online.
